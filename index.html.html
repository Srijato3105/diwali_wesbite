Sacred Mythological Heritage

The Origin of Diwali: The Ramayana Epic

Diwali is the living memory of a divine return, celebrating the victory of Dharma over Adharma, light over deep gloom, and truth over deceit.

Lord Rama, Goddess Sita & Lakshmana in forest exile
Chapter I

The Exile of Lord Rama

Lord Rama, the beloved crown prince of Ayodhya and embodiment of righteousness (Maryada Purushottam), dutifully accepted fourteen long years of exile into the Dandakaranya forest without a trace of resentment. His devoted consort Goddess Sita and loyal brother Lakshmana accompanied him into the wilderness.

Chapter II

The Abduction of Goddess Sita

While in the forest of Panchavati, Ravana abducted Goddess Sita by force and transported her to his fortress in Lanka. Lord Rama and Lakshmana journeyed south, forming an alliance with Sugriva and the devoted warrior Hanuman to cross the sea via the Ram Setu.

🏹

The Quest for Dharma

"Faith and determination bridged the tempestuous sea as the Vanara army inscribed Rama's sacred name on every floating stone."

Chapter III

The Victory Over Ravana

In a colossal war on the shores of Lanka, Lord Rama defeated Ravana with the Brahmashira weapon, restoring cosmic balance and rescuing Goddess Sita. Celebrated on Dussehra, twenty days before the return to Ayodhya!

First Diwali Celebration in Ayodhya
Chapter IV

The Return to Ayodhya

Returning on the moonless night of Kartik Amavasya, every citizen lit rows upon rows (Avali) of earthen lamps (Deepa) across balconies and river ghats to welcome Maryada Purushottam Rama home—birthing the eternal tradition of Deepavali.

Chapter V • Living Festival Today

Ayodhya: Where Millions of Lights Illuminate the Night

Experience the world-record breaking Ayodhya Deepotsav celebration, where over 2.5 million earthen diyas ignite the sacred Sarayu River ghats in an ethereal display of spiritual devotion.
